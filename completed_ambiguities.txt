resolved in the codebase.

1. UNIT RANGE DISCREPANCIES (RESOLVED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  WVIT | Math slot                                                    [DONE]
    Options: MATH 1261 (4u) vs MATH 1267 (3u)
    Resolution: Tile defaulted to 3u; updated to 4u so the flowchart total

    Options: MATH 1007 (3u) vs MATH 1261 (4u)

  EIM | Math slot                                                     [DONE]
    Options: MATH 1004 / MATH 1006 / MATH 1007 / MATH 1267
    Resolution: Tile default kept at 3u (matches MATH 1006/1007/1267 which
    are all 3u). Unit counts in eim_math_elective picker corrected:
      MATH 1004 Stretch College Algebra: 5u  (was incorrectly 3u)

      MATH 1007 Precalculus:             3u  (was incorrectly 4u)
      MATH 1267 Business Calculus:       3u  (was incorrectly 4u)

4. SLASH-CHOICE COURSES (Pick One of Two)                              [RESOLVED]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Resolution: expandSlashCourseNumber in frontend/lib/course-status.ts splits
"CHEM 2240/2242" → ["CHEM 2240", "CHEM 2242"], etc. The course lookup in
FlowchartGrid.tsx maps both the slash form and each component. Downstream tiles
whose only unmet prereqs are slash-choice tiles show ⚠️ prereq_warning instead
of 🔒 locked, indicating the student has options to fulfill the requirement.

Affected majors: MCRO, PLSC, CPE, ME, AERO, PSY, ENGL, AD, POLS, and others
with paired lecture/lab or or-choice requirements.
