Catalog Ambiguities & Known Limitations
========================================
Generated: 2026-05-18

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. UNIT RANGE DISCREPANCIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These slots model a fixed unit count but the catalog allows two options with
different units. The flowchart total may be slightly off depending on which
course a student takes.

  WVIT | Math slot
    Options: MATH 1261 (4u) vs MATH 1267 (3u)
    Issue: Modeled as 3u; a student taking Calc I would have 1 extra unit.

  KINE | Math slot
    Options: MATH 1007 (3u) vs MATH 1261 (4u)
    Issue: Same as WVIT — 1-unit variance depending on path.

  EIM | Math slot
    Options: MATH 1004 / MATH 1007
    Issue: Unit range 3–5u depending on placement; modeled at 3u.

2. FSN CULINOLOGY — CONFIRMED UNIT GAP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Culinology concentration lists FSN 3323 (Culinary Internship, 1u) in the
catalog but it doesn't fit cleanly into the 8-semester grid without pushing the
track to 121u. The concentration was modeled at 120u and this course is absent.
This is the only major with an unresolved catalog-to-flowchart unit mismatch.

3. SLASH-CHOICE COURSES (Pick One of Two)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tiles with a slash course_number (e.g., "CHEM 2240/2242") mean:
  - Other tiles cannot list them as prerequisites by exact course_number.
  - Affected downstream tiles have their prerequisites cleared to [].
  - The prerequisite chain is broken at that point in the flowchart.

  MCRO:
    - CHEM 2240/2242 (Organic Chem)
    - CHEM 3350/3352 (Biochemistry)
    - BIO 4461/4462/4463 (Senior Project)
    - BIO 1151/1153 (Intro Bio II)

  PLSC:
    - PLSC 1120/1120L (Principles of Plant Sciences with Lab)
      -> PLSC 3321 lost its prereq link to this tile

  CPE:
    - MATH 1151/2341
    - EE 3306/2328
    - STAT 3210/3310
    - WGQS 3350/3351

  ME:
    - IME 1141/1142/1149

  AERO:
    - IME 1143/1140

  Others: PSY, ENGL, AD, POLS (various or-choice support courses)

4. DYNAMIC ELECTIVE BUCKETS (BROAD APPROXIMATIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These _DYNAMIC elective pickers filter by dept + level range but do not
represent the exact approved course list from the catalog. Students should
verify with their advisor.

  hist_ud_elective       | All HIST 3000–4999
    Risk: Catalog may restrict to specific sub-areas; level filter over-includes.

  hist_global_elective   | All HIST 3000–4999
    Risk: Should exclude US-focused courses; no way to filter by topic.

  hist_ld_elective       | All HIST 2000–2999
    Risk: Catalog restricts to a specific subset of lower-div HIST options.

  coms_upper_div_elective| All COMS 3000–4999
    Risk: May over-include courses not approved for the requirement.

  pols_upper_div_elective| All POLS 3000–4000
    Risk: Catalog narrows to specific distribution areas within POLS.

  psy_social_personality / psy_mental_health / psy_cognitive
                         | All PSY 3000–4999
    Risk: Topic-area filters can't be enforced by level alone.

  phys_tech_elective     | PHYS/ASTR 3000–4999
    Risk: Actual catalog-approved list is only ~8 specific courses.

  plsc_concentration_elective | PLSC/SS/BOT 1000–4999
    Risk: Very broad; used as fallback when no concentration is selected.

  agb_cafes_prefix_elective   | 11 CAFES dept prefixes, any level
    Risk: Extremely broad; catalog has a specific approved list.

5. HIST — LANGUAGE AND UPPER-DIVISION SUPPORT REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HIST_LANG: "World Language Requirement"
    The catalog requires demonstrating proficiency in a foreign language but
    doesn't prescribe specific courses. Modeled as a 1-tile placeholder with
    no elective_key — the checklist shows no course options for this slot.

  HIST_GEUD4: "Non-History Social Science Upper Division"
    Catalog requires 3u UD Social Science from a non-HIST department. Modeled
    as a support-category placeholder with no elective_key; any qualifying UD
    social science course counts.

6. MCRO — SENIOR PROJECT SLOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIO 4461/4462/4463 (Senior Project) is a slash-choice among three options.
The elective picker (mcro_senior_project) lists all three as static options,
but students typically enroll in all three sequentially (1u + 1u + 2u), not
just one. The flowchart models this as a single 2u tile, which may undercount
the actual senior project commitment by 2 units.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PLSC — CONCENTRATION ELECTIVE UNIT MISMATCH WITHIN SLOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CON10 is 4u while CON1–9, CON11, CON12 are all 3u. The concentration-specific
pickers (plsc_fruit_crop_elective, plsc_environ_hort_elective,
plsc_plant_protection_elective) list courses of mixed units (3u and 4u). The
picker does not enforce which unit-size course goes into the 4u slot — a
student could put a 3u course into CON10 or a 4u course into a 3u slot.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. ECON — PROJECT LAB PAIRING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Many upper-division ECON electives come as a lecture+lab pair (e.g.,
ECON 3001 + ECON 3001L). The flowchart models these as a combined unit count,
but the _DYNAMIC picker only surfaces the lecture course. Students may not
realize they need to also register for the paired lab section.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. FREE / UNRESTRICTED ELECTIVE SLOTS (NO CATALOG BACKING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These placeholders have is_placeholder=True but no elective_key. The checklist
shows them as empty slots with no course options. This is by design for
unrestricted slots, but it means the checklist cannot help students discover
what courses qualify.

  POLS  : 6 free elective slots (POLS_FREE1–6)
  PSY   : 8 free elective slots
  ENGL  : 3 free elective slots
  HIST  : Language requirement slot (HIST_LANG)
  CS/SE/CPE/CE/ME/EE: Various "External Elective" and concentration slots
